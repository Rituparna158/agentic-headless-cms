The **Content Manager** is where your team writes and manages content. Once you have defined content types in the [Schema Builder](/docs/features/schema-builder), they appear here automatically as ready-to-use forms.

---

## Managing Entries

When you select a content type from the sidebar, you see all existing entries in a clean table:

- **Search and filter**: Quickly locate entries by title, status (draft or published), or last modified date.
- **Table sorting**: Click column headers to reorder entries by newest, oldest, or alphabetically.
- **Media previews**: If an entry includes an image or banner, its thumbnail displays directly in the list for quick visual recognition.

Click on any row to open the entry editor, or click **Create Entry** in the top right to start a new one.

---

## Editing Content

The editor adapts to your schema automatically, providing the right input for each field:

- **Rich Text**: Write formatted articles, documentation, or landing page copy with headings, lists, bold/italics, and links.
- **Media**: Upload new images or pick existing assets from the Media Library.
- **Relations**: Connect entries together, such as linking an author or category to a blog post.
- **Repeatable fields**: Add multiple items for fields like tags, bullet points, or gallery images.

---

## Version History & Restoring Previous Revisions

Every time you save or publish an entry, a new version is saved in the background.

To compare changes or restore older work:
1. Click **History** in the top toolbar to open the version list.
2. Select any previous version to see a side-by-side comparison of what was added or removed.
3. Click **Revert** to restore that version back to your editor.
